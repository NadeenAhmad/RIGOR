[Which tracks are in a specific playlist?]

The ontology can answer this by retrieving the TrackId values for a given PlaylistId from the PlaylistTrack table.

---

[Which playlists include a specific track?]

The ontology can answer this by retrieving the PlaylistId values for a given TrackId from the PlaylistTrack table.

---

[What are the unique combinations of PlaylistId and TrackId?]

The ontology can answer this by retrieving all unique pairs of PlaylistId and TrackId, as both are primary keys and thus form a composite unique key.

---

[How many tracks are in a specific playlist?]

The ontology can answer this by counting the number of records with a specific PlaylistId.

---

[Which tracks are not in a specific playlist?]

The ontology cannot answer this directly from the PlaylistTrack table alone, as it only contains information about tracks that are in playlists. Additional information about all available tracks would be needed.
