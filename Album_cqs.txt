[What is the unique identifier for each album in the database?]

The AlbumId field in the table uniquely identifies each album, as it is an integer and a primary key.

---

[Can an album have a null title?]

No, an album cannot have a null title, as the Title field is of type TEXT and is not nullable.

---

[What is the data type of the field that stores the artist associated with an album?]

The ArtistId field stores the artist associated with an album and is of type INTEGER.

---

[Is it possible for two albums to have the same title?]

Yes, it is possible for two albums to have the same title, as the Title field is not unique.

---

[What is the maximum length of an album title?]

The maximum length of an album title is 160 characters, as the Title field is of type NVARCHAR(160).
