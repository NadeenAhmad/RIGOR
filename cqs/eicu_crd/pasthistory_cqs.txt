[What types of past history notes are recorded for a patient?]

The ontology would answer this by querying the distinct values in the pasthistorynotetype column, which stores the types of past history notes.

[What is the value and text of a specific past history record for a given patient unit stay?]

The ontology would answer this by filtering the table for the specific patientunitstayid and retrieving the pasthistoryvalue and pasthistoryvaluetext columns.

The ontology would answer this by filtering the table for the specific pasthistoryid and retrieving the pasthistoryoffset and pasthistoryenteredoffset columns.

[What is the path associated with a specific past history note type?]

The ontology would answer this by filtering the table for the specific pasthistorynotetype and retrieving the pasthistorypath column.
