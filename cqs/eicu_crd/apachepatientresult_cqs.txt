What is the predicted ICU mortality rate for a patient with a specific Apache score?

The ontology would use the apachepatientresult table to join the apache score with the predicted ICU mortality field to answer this question.

---

How many patients had a pre-operative myocardial infarction (preopmi)?

The ontology would count the number of rows in the apachepatientresult table where the preopmi field is not null or has a value of 1.

---

What is the average length of stay in the hospital (unabridgedhosplos) for patients with a specific physician specialty?

The ontology would group the data by the physicianspeciality field and calculate the average of the unabridgedhosplos field for each group.

---

What is the predicted vs. actual ICU length of stay (predictediculos vs. actualiculos) for patients with a specific Apache version?

The ontology would filter the rows based on the apacheversion field and then compare the predictediculos and actualiculos fields.

---

How many patients had a PTCA within 24 hours (ptcawithin24h) of admission?

The ontology would count the number of rows in the apachepatientresult table where the ptcawithin24h field is not null or has a value of 1.
