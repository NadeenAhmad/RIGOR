[What clinical note types were documented for a given patient unit stay in the ICU?]

The ontology would answer this by retrieving note records for the specified patientunitstayid and returning the distinct NOTETYPE values, which represent the categories of clinical documentation such as nursing notes, physician progress notes, or procedure notes recorded during the stay.

---

[What is the clinical content documented in a specific note for a given patient unit stay?]

The ontology would answer this by retrieving the NOTETEXT for the specified patientunitstayid and NOTETYPE, returning the free-text clinical narrative recorded by the care team for that note category.

---

[At what point during the ICU stay was a specific type of clinical note entered for a given patient?]

The ontology would answer this by retrieving NOTEENTEREDOFFSET for the specified patientunitstayid filtered by NOTETYPE, indicating the time in minutes from ICU admission at which the clinical note was documented.

---

[Which patients had clinical notes of a specific type documented during their ICU stay?]

The ontology would answer this by filtering note records by NOTETYPE and returning the corresponding patientunitstayid values linked to the patient entity, enabling identification of patients for whom that documentation category exists.

---

[What structured note path and value were recorded alongside the free-text note for a given patient unit stay and note type?]

The ontology would answer this by retrieving NOTEPATH and NOTEVALUE together with NOTETEXT for the specified patientunitstayid and NOTETYPE, combining structured classification with narrative content for the clinical note.
