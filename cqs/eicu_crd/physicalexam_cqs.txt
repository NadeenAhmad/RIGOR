[What physical examination findings were documented for a given patient unit stay?]

The ontology would answer this by retrieving physicalexam records for the specified patientunitstayid and returning physicalexampath and physicalexamvalue together, which capture the body system examined and the corresponding clinical finding.

---

[What is the physical examination finding for a specific body system or clinical parameter for a given patient unit stay?]

The ontology would answer this by filtering physicalexam records for the specified patientunitstayid and physicalexampath and returning physicalexamvalue and physicalexamtext, providing both the structured value and the narrative description of the examination finding.

---

[Which patients had abnormal physical examination findings documented in a specific clinical domain such as respiratory, neurological, or cardiovascular assessment?]

The ontology would answer this by filtering physicalexam records where physicalexampath matches the specified clinical domain and physicalexamvalue indicates an abnormal finding, returning the corresponding patientunitstayid values linked to the patient entity.

---

[At what point during the ICU stay was a physical examination performed for a given patient?]

The ontology would answer this by retrieving physicalexamoffset for the specified patientunitstayid, indicating the time in minutes from ICU admission at which the physical examination was documented.

---

[How do physical examination findings relate to the patient's vital signs and laboratory results at a comparable time point during the ICU stay?]

The ontology would answer this by linking physicalexam records via patientunitstayid to vitalperiodic and lab records using comparable observationoffset or labresultoffset values, enabling integrated clinical assessment combining examination findings with physiological measurements.
