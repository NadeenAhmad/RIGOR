[Which patients had a complication-free postoperative course?]

The ontology would answer this by retrieving complication records where complication_free_progression = 1 and following the patient_id relation to the corresponding patients.

---

[Which patients had postoperative complications classified as severe according to the Clavien-Dindo system?]

The ontology would answer this by linking complication.clavien_dindo to the clavien_dindo table and retrieving patients whose complications map to clinically severe grades such as Grade III, IV, or V.

---

[Which patients developed postoperative decubitus and what severity grade was recorded?]

The ontology would answer this by identifying complication records with decubitus = 1, linking decubitus_score to dekubitus_grade, and returning both the patient and the associated grade.

---

[Which patients required reintubation, and what were their ventilation and inpatient-stay durations?]

The ontology would answer this by selecting complication records with reintubation = 1 and retrieving the related duration_of_inpatient_stay_d, ventilation_duration_h, and noninvasive_ventilation_duration_h values.

---

[Which patients underwent postoperative endoscopic management such as ERCP, and what related endoscopy information was documented?]

The ontology would answer this by retrieving complication records where endoscopic_retrograde_cholangiopancreatography = 1 or endoscopy = 1 and returning the associated endoscopy_text and patient linkage.
