[What clinical severity of pressure ulcer is represented by each Dekubitus grade?]

The ontology would answer this by modeling each dekubitus_grade entry as a pressure-ulcer severity class using the dekubitus_grade_stadium values such as Grad I through Grad IV.

[Which patients had postoperative decubitus and what pressure-ulcer grade was recorded?]

The ontology would answer this by identifying complication records with decubitus = 1, linking decubitus_score to dekubitus_grade, and returning the corresponding severity stage.

The ontology would answer this by following the complication.decubitus_score foreign key to dekubitus_grade and selecting patients whose linked stage is Grad III or Grad IV.

[What pressure-ulcer grade is associated with a given complication record?]

The ontology would answer this by mapping the complication record's decubitus_score to the referenced dekubitus_grade entry and returning the dekubitus_grade_stadium value.
