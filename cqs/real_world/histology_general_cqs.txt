[What is the histological diagnosis for a given patient?]

The ontology can answer this question by querying the 'histological_diagnosis' column based on the 'patient_id'.

[Which pathologist made a diagnosis for a given order number?]

The ontology can answer this question by retrieving the 'pathologist' column for the given 'order_number'.

[What is the summary of histology for a specific patient?]

The ontology can answer this question by querying the 'histology_summary' column based on the 'patient_id'.

---

[How many unique pathologists have made diagnoses?]

The ontology can answer this question by counting the distinct values in the 'pathologist' column.
