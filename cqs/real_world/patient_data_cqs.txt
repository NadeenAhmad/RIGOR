[What sex category and age at surgery are recorded for a given patient?]

The ontology would answer this by retrieving the patient's patient_data record, linking the sex field to the sex table, and returning both the human-readable sex category and age_at_surgery.

---

[Which patients belong to a given sex category, such as weiblich, männlich, or divers?]

The ontology would answer this by linking patient_data.sex to sex.sex_id and retrieving all patient_data records associated with the selected sex category.

---

[What is the relationship between a patient's current identifier and old identifier?]

The ontology would answer this by using the patient_data record to relate patient_id to patient_id_old as alternative identifiers for the same patient entity.

---

[Which patients were 60 years or older at the time of surgery, and what sex category was recorded for them?]

The ontology would answer this by filtering patient_data records on age_at_surgery >= 60 and linking each record to the referenced sex classification.

---

[When was a patient's demographic record created, and what demographic attributes were recorded in that record?]

The ontology would answer this by retrieving the patient_data entry for the given patient and returning created_at together with age_at_surgery and the linked sex category.
